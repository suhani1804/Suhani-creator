About

I'm Suhani, a Canada-based creator who turns places, products and everyday moments into content worth stopping for.

Travel is where my content lives today, but I'm curious about anything that looks good on camera: hotels and cafés, fashion and accessories, gadgets, luggage, wellness, home and more. I'm still discovering which worlds I love working in most, and I'd rather explore them with brands than rule anything out in advance.

My default style is visual and cinematic, with natural brand integration instead of a hard sell. When a brief calls for it, I'm just as happy on camera, with voiceover, or telling a story.

My approach

Whatever the category, the process is the same.

Starting points I'd bring to a brief. Every campaign is shaped with you.

Hotels, cafés & venues

Arrival, atmosphere, details and the moment people will want to book. Natural light, slow pacing, a Reel plus photography.

Fashion, accessories & luggage

Product in motion through real days and journeys, with close details that show finish, fit and how it's used.

Tech & gadgets

Clean, aesthetic scenes that show the product in everyday life, visual-first, or with a voiceover walkthrough if you prefer.

Destinations & experiences

Cinematic establishing shots, local details and a vertical photo set that also suits your own channels.

Something not on this list

I'm open to new categories. Tell me about your brand and I'll come back with a creative direction.
